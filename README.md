# BacktestSaham
BacktestSaham is an interactive dashboard featuring over 100,000 backtests on Indonesian stocks. Unlike most backtesting platforms, BacktestSaham offers a screening feature that allows users to create custom filters based on backtest metrics such as Sharpe Ratio, Total Trades, Return, and more. BacktestSaham also provides two preset filters that users can apply for long-term and short-term strategies. The goal of BacktestSaham is to provide Indonesian traders with comprehensive and robust backtesting data to help them identify trading strategies that best fit their individual trading styles.

BacktestSaham is suitable for both beginners and advanced traders. To learn more about what a backtest is, as well as the common backtest metrics, you can visit the Reports section on BacktestSaham. At the end of the Reports section, I also include two recommended indicators along with their optimal settings that have shown the best performance in the Indonesian stock market.   

## Development
